## Description

A 600-year-old manuscript sits inside Yale's Beinecke Library—and no one can read it with scholarly agreement. The Voynich Manuscript contains impossible plants, circular skies, human figures, and thousands of words written in an unidentified script.

But what does the evidence actually prove?

In this episode of RAHASYA: Global Mysteries, we separate fact from speculation: the 1404–1438 parchment date, the evidence for five scribal hands, the manuscript's trail from Prague to Rome and Yale, the language-like patterns in its text, and the four theories that still compete to explain it.
