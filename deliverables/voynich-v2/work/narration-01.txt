Three facts make this book almost impossible to dismiss.

First: its parchment was made more than six centuries ago. Second: at least five different people appear to have written it. Third: after generations of cryptographers, linguists, historians, and computer scientists, no one can read a single page with scholarly agreement.

Inside Yale's Beinecke Library, the object is cataloged simply as Manuscript 408. Its two hundred thirty-four pages contain impossible plants, rotating skies, human figures, and thousands of words in an alphabet found nowhere else.

If it is a cipher, the key has vanished. If it is a language, every known speaker is gone. And if it is a hoax, someone invested extraordinary time, skill, and money to create a lie that has survived for six hundred years.

This is not the story of a solved code. It is the evidence behind the book no one can read.
